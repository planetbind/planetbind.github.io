Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a question? Email planetbind@gmail.com — from inside the app, Profile ▸ Support has a "Report a bug" button that pre-fills your device and version details for you.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Stillpoint never asks you to sign in or create an account — everything works immediately, and your data stays on your device.

What does Stillpoint Premium unlock?

The full ambient sound library and saved mixes, every live background, all Drift visual variants, Flow's Fluid Studio customization, and every alternate app icon. Every practice — Breathe, Flow, Drift, and Sounds — already works for free.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your device: Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Stillpoint. Or inside the app: Profile ▸ Stillpoint Premium ▸ Manage.

Does Stillpoint work offline?

Yes — the entire app works with no internet connection, including subscription status once it's synced at least once.

How do I delete my data?

Profile ▸ Clear practice history removes every logged session. Deleting the app removes everything else Stillpoint stored, since nothing is kept anywhere else.
