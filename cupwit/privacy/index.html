Cupwit Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Cupwit is made by Planetbind LLC, a Washington, USA company (“we”).

The short version

Cupwit has no accounts, ads or tracking. We don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t collect anything that identifies you. Your play stays on your iPhone or iPad.

What stays on your device

Your name and pin (if you choose them), your results, ratings, streaks, saved games and settings are stored only on your device, in Cupwit’s own storage. We can’t see them. Deleting the app deletes them, and Settings › Reset all progress erases your results and saved games.

Anonymous stats (optional)

With Settings › Share anonymous stats on (it can be turned off at any time), Cupwit can count which games are opened, finished, quit partway, replayed, starred and rated, with rough times such as “1–3 minutes” and the reason you pick for a low rating. These counts never include your name, pin, scores, answers, contacts, location, advertising identifier or anything else that identifies you, and they are never used for ads or combined with data from other apps. Today these counts stay on your device. Before any are sent, we will update this policy to name the analytics service and where it stores data.

Standings (optional, Apple Game Center)

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, Cupwit sends your daily-set points and a code for your chosen pin to Game Center so friends and other players can see them on leaderboards. Apple operates Game Center and holds your Game Center name, friends and scores under Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can stop sending scores at any time in Settings › Show me in Standings, or sign out of Game Center in your device’s Settings. Scores already posted stay on Apple’s boards until the board resets.

Daily reminder

The optional reminder is a notification scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

Feedback and email

Send feedback opens an email you can read and change before sending. Below your message it lists the app version, days played, best streak and your average fun rating per game. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to fix problems, and we delete them on request.

Children

Cupwit is suitable for all ages. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, write to us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Because we don’t hold personal information about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control everything on your device: turn off anonymous stats or Standings in Settings, erase progress with Reset all progress, or delete the app. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to see or delete that email. Wherever you live (including the EU, UK and California), you can contact us with any privacy request and we will respond within 30 days.

Security and retention

Data on your device is protected by your device’s own encryption. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its date, and describe any new kind of data before the app starts using it. Questions: hello@planetbind.com.
