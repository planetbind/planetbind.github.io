Cupwit Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email hello@planetbind.com. We usually reply within two business days. In the app, Settings › Send feedback starts an email with the details that help us fix things.

Common questions

How do I learn a game? Every game opens on its card. Tap How to play for a short practice round; when it ends, or you tap Skip, the real game starts.

What’s in Today’s set? A daily puzzle (Ledger on weekdays, Balance at weekends) and three quick games from different kinds (Logic, Words, Math, Memory), with a board puzzle in the weekend set. The games take turns, so each comes round regularly and none repeats the next day. Everyone gets the same set. Finishing any one game keeps your streak going.

I left a puzzle halfway. Is it saved? Yes. Puzzles save as you play and appear under Games › Continue playing for up to 7 days (the newest 5). Open one to continue or start over. Memory games always start fresh, because the remembering is the game.

Why is the daily set the same for everyone? So Standings are fair: daily puzzles are Medium and the other daily games use one shared level. In free play from Games, pick Easy, Medium or Hard, and the other games adjust to how you play.

How do Standings work? Sign in to Game Center on the Standings tab or in Settings. Your first try at each daily game scores up to 1,000 points. Hints lower a round’s score, and a puzzle filled in by hints scores nothing. Turn off Settings › Show me in Standings to stop sending scores.

What do ratings mean? Each game has a rating from 0 to 1,000 that follows your recent rounds. Strong rounds at harder levels raise it most. See Settings › How scoring works. Ratings describe how you play Cupwit’s games, nothing more.

How do I reset or delete my data? Settings › Reset all progress erases streaks, ratings, levels, history and saved games on this device. Your name, pin and settings stay. Deleting the app removes everything. We don’t keep a copy.

My progress is gone after changing phones. Cupwit keeps progress only on the device, with no account, so it doesn’t move to a new phone unless you restore from a backup of the old one.
