Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Stillpoint collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no network connection required to use the app.

What stays on your device

Everything Stillpoint stores — your practice history, streaks, preferences, saved sound mixes, and reminder time — is saved locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage frameworks (SwiftData and UserDefaults). None of it is transmitted anywhere, because Stillpoint has no server to send it to.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, it is scheduled entirely on your device through Apple's local notification system. Stillpoint never sees or stores where you are or when your phone is unlocked to decide when to notify you.

Health data

Stillpoint can optionally write Mindful Minutes to Apple Health for completed sessions. This requires your explicit permission via Apple's system permission prompt, uses Apple's HealthKit framework exclusively, and nothing is shared with us or any third party.

Subscriptions

Stillpoint Premium is an auto-renewable subscription processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Stillpoint never receives your payment details — Apple handles billing, and your subscription entitlement is verified on-device. No account with Stillpoint is created or required to subscribe.

Third parties

Stillpoint contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Children's privacy

Stillpoint does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional cloud sync feature — this page will be updated first, and any new data collection will be clearly disclosed and, where required, opt-in.
